ember

A 3D and 2D game engine for the browser in TypeScript and WebGL2, with no dependencies. It has an editor for scenes, pixel-art sprites with frame animation, and scripts attached to objects.

EditorBuild a scene, draw and animate sprites, write scripts, and press Play. Crystal RunA 3D demo with physics, shadows and lights. Moss LedgeA 2D platformer on the sprite and tilemap layer. Stress test1,500 extra physics crates.

Source: github.com/vaughnponcee/ember
